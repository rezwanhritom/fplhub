About FPL Hub

FPL Hub is a fantasy Premier League companion built around live data synced into your own database. Track form, compare players and clubs, and shape your squad with tools that stay close to the pitch.

What you can do

  • Build and save a fantasy squad on the dashboard
  • Filter deep player statistics by club and metric
  • Compare players and teams side by side
  • Spot the best fixtures for players and clubs ahead

Data

Season data is pulled from Fantasy Premier League sources and stored locally so the hub stays fast and under your control.
