Cookie policy

FPL Hub uses cookies and similar storage mainly to keep you signed in and remember session state while you use the dashboard and tools.

Essential cookies

Session cookies are required for login, logout, and protecting authenticated requests. Without them, squad saving and account features will not work.

Analytics

We do not currently run third-party advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out of FPL Hub.
